Who we are

Built for people who sell through relationships

BlueChimp is a relationship intelligence platform for B2B sales teams, consultants, agencies and founders. It shows which companies are researching solutions like yours, who in your network can introduce you, and what to say when you reach out.

BlueChimp is built and operated by Findable LLC. We started as a small tool our founder built to find work through warm introductions instead of cold applications. When other people asked to use it, we turned it into a product for anyone who sells through relationships.

Our story

Why BlueChimp exists

In 2025, founder Israel Kloss spent nine months looking for work: roughly 700 applications, four interviews and no offers. So he changed his approach. He looked for companies showing real signs of need, then used his LinkedIn network to find a warm way in.

He built a small tool to do it, and it worked. Soon other people asked to use the same tool, and sales leaders asked for a version for their teams. That tool became BlueChimp.

How our data works

Multiple signals. One clearer picture.

BlueChimp combines intent signals from G2, Apollo, Bombora, Explorium and SalesIntel with proprietary LinkedIn signals. Sources are merged and de-duplicated, then accounts are scored by buying intent, relationship strength and likely buyer fit.
